# Technical Review - Paper v1

**Reviewer role:** independent research manager; not the manuscript author.  
**Population:** Paper v1 public-export candidate and its claim ledger.  
**Verdict:** PASS WITH BOUNDED NON-CLAIMS.

## Checks

- The contribution is framed as governed integration, not invention of each component.
- The six central claims remain hypotheses.
- Proposed Kernel, Studio, Domain Engine, and reference-corporation work is not described as implemented.
- The bounded Auto-Refresh result is separated from 20-cycle, fleet, universal-platform, and business-outcome claims.
- DeepSeek Harness is represented as an event-sourced same-session runtime with automatic compaction and a durable full log.
- The capsule-versus-compaction comparison is explicitly a future experiment, not an empirical superiority claim.
- Authority, identity, runtime binding, memory, and evidence remain separate.
- Honest terminals and non-self-activation remain explicit.

## Maximum technical disposition

Paper v1 is fit for public release as architecture and research synthesis. It is not an empirical systems paper and must not be represented as one.
