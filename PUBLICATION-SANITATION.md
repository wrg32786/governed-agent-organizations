# Publication Sanitation

The private research archive is not the public artifact. This repository is the clean export.

## Working-tree review

The candidate export was reviewed for:

- personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, and addresses;
- private repository, issue, pull-request, review, and comment links;
- inaccessible internal commit identifiers used as public evidence;
- session, machine, seat, fleet, and host identifiers;
- local filesystem paths and usernames;
- customer, sponsor, employee, financial, or private business information;
- screenshots, attachments, and hidden document metadata;
- private instructions and personal quotations;
- third-party code, diagrams, tables, and prose beyond their terms.

The continuity case study now names only public product identities. Private candidate and program identities were removed. Detailed raw evidence remains private and is explicitly listed as a limitation.

## Attribution and licensing

- Exact public source pins are recorded.
- MCP's mixed Apache-2.0/MIT/CC-BY-4.0 transition is preserved.
- MemHarness's exact-pin LICENSE is Apache-2.0; its README badge says MIT, and the inconsistency is recorded.
- The Cordis preprint is citation-only because no reuse license was found.
- No third-party prose, figures, diagrams, PDF pages, or code are reproduced.

## Rendered artifacts

The HTML and PDF are generated from the sanitized `PAPER.md`. The PDF metadata is limited to the paper title and subject; no private author email, local path, or machine identifier is embedded.

## History

The current repository remains the private review and forensic archive. Its historical branches, pull-request refs, issue and pull-request history, commit ancestry, and reachable objects may contain private author metadata or internal operating history.

Public release must use a genuinely fresh repository/object database populated only from the unchanged parentless clean root. The only alternative is a GitHub-confirmed complete purge of all old refs and objects followed by fresh independent review of the resulting repository.

Rewriting a branch, moving the default branch, or changing this repository's visibility is insufficient and is not an authorized publication procedure.

## Final human review

The final principal decision should inspect the frozen commit, HTML, PDF, outbound links, CSV files, Git log, release archive, and attribution notices. Publication is not authorized until that decision is recorded.
