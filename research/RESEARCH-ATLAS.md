## Maturity ladder

```text
L0 question
L1 source-grounded observation
L2 independently dispositioned research/design
L3 promoted implementation contract
L4 implemented mechanism
L5 independently demonstrated behavior
L6 repeated operating result
L7 measured business outcome
```

Most first-edition research is L1-L2. The bounded Auto-Refresh transport claim reaches L5 for one named population. No fleet L6 result or L7 business outcome is claimed.
